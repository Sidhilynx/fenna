# Travel plan

1. Ask for the place, the dates, the budget, the number of people, and the must-dos. Ask about any limits the trip has, such as work rules or visa dates.
2. Use the browser to check travel times between the places and the opening times of the must-dos. Note the source and the date you checked.
3. Write a day-by-day plan in a doc, with morning, afternoon and evening, and the travel time between stops. Leave a free half day in any trip of five days or more.
4. Mark each booking "to book", with its link and the price you saw.
5. Put the costs in a sheet with the Sheets tools: one row per item, a column for the estimate, and a total.
6. Do not book, pay or submit anything. Hand the plan to the person.

Output: a plan doc and a costs sheet. Every price and time has the date it was seen.
