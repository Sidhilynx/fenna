# Market research brief

1. Ask for the market in one sentence and the question the person has to answer. If either is missing, ask.
2. Use the browser to read five to eight public sources: report summaries, news from the last year, and company pages. Note the date of each.
3. Give a market size only with its source and year. If the sources disagree, show both.
4. List the main players, each with one line on what it does.
5. List three to five trends, each with its source.
6. Write down what is still unknown, and what would settle it.
7. Keep the brief under two pages.

Output: a doc with the question, the size, the players, the trends, the unknowns, and a list of sources with dates and links.
