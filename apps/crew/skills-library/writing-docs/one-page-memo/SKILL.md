# One-page memo

1. Ask for the decision needed, who decides, and by when. If any of these is missing, ask before writing.
2. Write the memo in this order: the decision asked for in one sentence; background in three sentences at most; two or three options, each with a line on cost and risk; your recommendation and its reason; what happens after a yes.
3. Keep it under 500 words, with plain headings.
4. Mark each number with its source, or as "estimate".
5. End with the date the decision is needed by.

Output: one doc that fits on one printed page.
