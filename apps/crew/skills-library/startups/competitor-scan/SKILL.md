# Competitor scan

1. Ask for the product in one sentence, and for the competitors the person already knows. If none are named, find five to eight others with the browser.
2. For each competitor, open its home page, its pricing page and its product page in the browser. Read them. Do not log in or fill in forms.
3. For each competitor, record what it sells, who it is for, its price range, one thing it does better and one thing it does worse. Put the link to the page beside each fact.
4. If a fact is not on a public page, write "not found". Do not guess.
5. Put the results in a sheet with the Sheets tools: one row per competitor, one column per fact, and a column for the links.
6. Write three to five lines on where the person can stand out, based only on the facts you found.

Output: a comparison sheet, and a doc with the three to five lines. Every fact in the sheet has a link.
