# Customer interview notes

1. Read the notes or transcript the person gives you, from Files or from a meeting record.
2. Write a short header: the customer's role and company size, the date, and what the interview was for. Leave out contact details.
3. List the problems the customer described, in their own words, each with the quote that shows it. Note how often they came up and how strongly they were said.
4. List what the customer does now to solve each problem, and what it costs them in time or money.
5. Note anything they asked for, and anything they said they would pay for.
6. End with the one question to ask the next customer.
7. Keep the customer's name out of the notes unless the person asks to keep it.

Output: one doc per interview with the header, the problems and quotes, the current workarounds, the asks, and the next question. If the person keeps a sheet of all interviews, add one row for this one with the Sheets tools.
