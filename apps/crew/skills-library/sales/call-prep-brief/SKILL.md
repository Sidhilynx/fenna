# Call prep brief

1. Read the earlier emails, meeting notes and the account row in Files or in a sheet, if there are any.
2. Open the prospect's company site and its recent news with the browser. Note three facts, each with its link.
3. Write who will be on the call and what each person cares about. Use only the sources you have read.
4. List what the buyer has already said, and what is still unknown.
5. Write five questions to ask. Start with open questions, and aim each one at something unknown.
6. Note any next step the person wants agreed before the call ends.

Output: a one-page doc titled with the company and the date, with the sections: who, context, what we know, what we do not know, questions, and the ask.
