# Follow-up after a meeting

1. Read the meeting notes or transcript from the meetings record or from Files.
2. List what was agreed: the decisions, the owners and the dates. Mark anything unclear as "to confirm".
3. Write the email: one line of thanks, the three main points the customer raised, the agreed next steps with owners and dates, and one question if something is still open.
4. Keep it under 150 words. Use the names the customer used for things.
5. Do not promise a feature, price or date that is not in the notes.
6. Put the email in a doc for the person to send.

Output: one email in a doc. At the top, list any "to confirm" items so the person can fix them before sending.
