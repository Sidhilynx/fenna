# Cold email sequence

1. Ask for the buyer's role, the problem the product solves for them, and one proof point with a number. If there is no proof point, write the emails without one. Never invent one.
2. Write three emails over about two weeks. The first names the problem and asks one question. The second gives a short proof point and asks a different question. The third is a clear close that leaves the door open.
3. Each email makes one ask and stays under 120 words. Use the buyer's own words where the notes have them.
4. Write a subject line of six words or fewer for each email.
5. Leave the name, the company and the link as placeholders in brackets.
6. Do not claim results, customers or awards that are not in the notes.

Output: one doc with the three emails, their subject lines, and the wait before each one.
