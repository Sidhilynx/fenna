# Newsletter draft

1. If there is a past issue in Files, read it to match its length and voice.
2. Read this period's changes, posts and customer news in Files, or in the doc the person names.
3. Choose at most three items. Put the most useful one first.
4. For each item, write a headline, two or three sentences, and one link or next step.
5. Write a short opening line in the person's voice, and a closing line that asks for one reply or one action.
6. Keep the issue under 350 words.
7. Check each link and date against the notes.

Output: one doc titled with the issue number and the month, ready to paste into the person's email tool.
