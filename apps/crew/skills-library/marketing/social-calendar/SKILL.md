# Social calendar

1. Read the month's news, launches and events in Files, or ask the person for them.
2. Plan only for the channels the person already uses.
3. Plan three to five posts a week per channel. Leave one day each week free for replies.
4. For each post, write one row: date, channel, the news it comes from, the post text, the link, and the status "draft".
5. Vary the kind of post: one teaching, one showing the product, one from a customer, one asking a question.
6. Keep each post within the channel's length.
7. Write the rows into a sheet with the Sheets tools, with a header row.

Output: a sheet with one row per post, and a short note at the top on the month's theme.
