# The Fenna name and logo

Fenna's code is free under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE): you may use, change and
share it. The **name "Fenna"**, the app names (Fenna Slides, Fenna Docs,
Fenna Sheets, Fenna Whiteboard) and the **icons** are not covered by that
license. They tell people that what they're installing comes from us.

## Fine without asking

- Saying your work runs on, works with, or is built from Fenna ("a theme for
  Fenna Slides", "forked from Fenna").
- Writing about Fenna, reviewing it, teaching it.
- Sharing unchanged installers from our releases, with the name as it is.

## Ask first

- Shipping a changed version (a fork) under the name Fenna or with our
  icons. Please give your fork its own name and icons; you can still say it
  is based on Fenna.
- Using the name or icons in a product name, company name or domain.
- Selling anything with the name or icons on it.

Open an issue to ask; we say yes to most reasonable requests.
