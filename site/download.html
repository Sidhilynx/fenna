Unsigned for now: how to open on first start

The installers are not code-signed yet, so your operating system will display an alert on first launch.

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag the app to your /Applications folder.
  2. Double-click to open. When macOS warns that the app cannot be opened, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section, and click Open Anyway.
xattr -cr "/Applications/Fenna Workspace.app"
  1. Launch Fenna-*-setup.exe.
  2. When the blue "Windows protected your PC" SmartScreen alert appears, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway to start installation.
  1. Make the downloaded .AppImage executable:
chmod +x Fenna-*.AppImage && ./Fenna-*.AppImage
