Five editors

The editors your bots work in.

  • Fenna Slides

    Decks and presentations, with present mode and PowerPoint, PDF and web export.

  • Fenna Docs

    Memos, plans and notes, with tabs, comments and suggested edits, and Word and Markdown export.

  • Fenna Sheets

    Spreadsheets with formulas, charts and conditional formatting, and Excel and CSV import and export.

  • Fenna Whiteboard

    Brainstorms, flowcharts and retros on an infinite canvas.

  • Fenna Design

    A canvas of artboards for screens, landing pages, posters and social posts, exported to PNG, SVG and PDF.

  • Each is also its own free app that any AI can drive through MCP.
