Workflow

Designed for autonomous agents

01 / INSTALL

Run Fenna Workspace

A single desktop app for Windows, macOS, or Linux. No account, no login screen, and no remote servers.

02 / CONNECT

Connect your agent

Open the built-in MCP guide to copy the configuration for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, or Antigravity.

03 / BUILD

Ask it to make something

Prompt your agent to outline slides, draft docs, compute sheets, or arrange boards. Edits land live on your disk.

Principles

Built on plain truths

Plain folders on your disk

Every deck, doc, sheet, board, and design is stored as human-readable HTML and JSON files in folders on your machine. No lock-in; your files outlive the app.

No account, no analytics

Fenna has no login and collects no analytics. The app only checks GitHub once a day for releases. Read our full privacy policy.

Works with local MCP agents

Tested with Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, and Antigravity over local stdio. Your agent inspects and modifies files using strict schema validation.

GNU AGPLv3 open source

Free and open-source software under the AGPL-3.0 license. You can inspect every line, audit every network request, and build it from source.

Unsigned for now: how to open on first start

The installers are not code-signed yet, so your operating system will display an alert on first launch.

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Fenna Workspace to your /Applications folder.
  2. Double-click to open. When macOS warns that the app cannot be opened, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section, and click Open Anyway.
xattr -cr "/Applications/Fenna Workspace.app"
  1. Launch Fenna-Workspace-*-setup.exe.
  2. When the blue "Windows protected your PC" SmartScreen alert appears, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway to start installation.
  1. Make the downloaded .AppImage executable:
chmod +x Fenna-Workspace-*.AppImage && ./Fenna-Workspace-*.AppImage
