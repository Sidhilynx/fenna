New: Builder

Ask for an app. Builder makes it, tests it, then uses it for you.

Every crew now comes with Builder. Say what you want. It builds the app on its own computer, clicks through it to check that it works, and gives itself a tool to use it.

BuilderBuilds apps, tools and small websites
  1. Build me a habit tracker.
  3. Wrote Apps/habits/app.json
  4. Wrote Apps/habits/index.html
  5. Opened Habits and clicked through it
  6. Habits is on my desktop. It has a tool, tick, so I can use it for you.
  7. Tick reading for today.
  8. app__habits__tick reading
  9. Done. Reading is ticked for today, 4 days in a row.
