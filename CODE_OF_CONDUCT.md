# Code of Conduct

Fenna follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; explain rather than dismiss.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, or personal attacks,
  in public or in private.
- Don't publish anyone's private information without their permission.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainers privately at the email address on the
[Sidhilynx GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sidhilynx). Reports are read
only by the maintainers and kept confidential. The maintainers decide what
happens, from a private word to a permanent ban, following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
